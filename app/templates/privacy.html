1. Data Minimization

We collect only the minimum data necessary for tournament matchmaking: chosen username, city, preferred language, and linked public external game IDs. We do not collect government identity cards, exact birth dates, or payment details.

2. Minors Safeguarding

GameBridge does not permit real-money gambling, adult content, or predatory microtransactions. Players under 18 participate in free community gaming with parent/guardian guidance.

3. Data Deletion & Export

Users may request complete account deletion or data export by contacting the platform administrator. Public passport URLs are immediately revoked upon account deactivation.

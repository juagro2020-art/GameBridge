1. Free Grassroots Competitions

All tournaments on GameBridge Ethiopia are completely free to enter. GameBridge does not charge entry fees, process real-money payments, or operate gambling activities. Prizing consists of platform ladder Elo, city rankings, community trophies, and sponsor awards.

2. Fair Play & Competitive Integrity

Players must compete using their own linked accounts. Using chess engines, aimbots, smurf accounts, or submitting fabricated result screenshots is strictly prohibited and results in permanent ladder disqualification.

3. Append-Only Competition Records

Match outcomes, bracket advancements, and Elo events are recorded as immutable business events. While personal identifiers may be deleted upon account closure, mathematical bracket history is retained for competition integrity.
